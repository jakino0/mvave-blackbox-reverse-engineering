# Security and device-safety policy

This repository is for interoperability research on hardware owned or
authorized by the researcher.

- Read-only analysis is the default.
- Runtime experiments should be reversible and narrowly scoped.
- Flashing modified firmware is out of scope for the current research phase.
- Global audio-output patches are not acceptable for the local-monitor goal.
- Remove serial numbers, addresses, credentials, and personal paths before
  sharing logs.

For a potentially dangerous finding, open a minimal issue without a weaponized
payload or contact the maintainers privately once a contact method is listed.
